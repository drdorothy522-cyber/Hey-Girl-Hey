Browsing Safely

Computers and phones store a record of the sites you visit. If someone monitors your device, they may be able to see you were here. Use the Quick Exit button at the bottom of every page to leave this site instantly.

  • Use a safer device. A friend's phone, a library computer, or a work computer.
  • Use Quick Exit. The red button at the bottom of every page leaves this site instantly.
  • Clear your history. Delete your browser history and cache after visiting.
  • Use private browsing. Incognito or Private mode doesn't save history on that device.
  • Trust your instincts. If you think you're being monitored, you probably are.
