About Us

Faith • Identity • Purpose • GODfidence

Our Purpose

H.E.Y. G.I.R.L., Helping Every Young Girl in "REAL LIFE," is a Bible-based organization grounded in faith, love, respect, and positivity. We exist to empower, equip, and inspire underserved girls and young women throughout every stage of life to discover WHO she is, understand WHOSE she is, and become the limitless, PHENOMENAL woman God has designed her to be.

Through faith-based mentorship, guidance, encouragement, and meaningful community connections, we help girls build an unshakeable foundation in God, walk boldly in their identity and purpose, and thrive in "REAL LIFE." Equipped to navigate challenges with faith and resilience. Rooted in God. Confident in who she is. Secure in whose she is. Bold in who she is becoming.

Our program is designed to love, encourage, support, and empower girls and young women as they grow in confidence, leadership, GODfidence, and purpose.

Our Mission

To walk alongside girls and women through faith-based mentorship, guidance, and support, creating safe spaces where they can grow in confidence, develop life and leadership skills, pursue their goals, and navigate REAL LIFE with purpose. Equipped to lead, serve, inspire, and make a lasting impact.

Our Vision

To ignite a global, faith-centered movement across generations, empowering girls and women to know WHO they are, understand WHOSE they are, embrace their God-given gifts, and boldly lead, serve, inspire, and transform their families, communities, and the world.
