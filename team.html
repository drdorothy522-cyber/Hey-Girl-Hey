Our Team

Meet the women behind the Movement. Led by women of faith, experience, and testimony.

Officers

President & CEO

Dr. Dorothy LaShay White, DNP

Visionary Leader · Healthcare Professional · Published Author · Woman of Faith

Dr. Dorothy LaShay White, DNP, is a visionary leader, healthcare professional, published author, and woman of faith whose life and leadership are rooted in prayer, purpose, resilience, service, and empowerment.

As President & CEO of H.E.Y. G.I.R.L. the Movement, Helping Every Young Girl in "REAL LIFE," Dr. Dorothy is passionate about creating spaces where girls and young women are seen, heard, supported, and equipped to thrive.

With 18 years of nursing experience and leadership ranging from the bedside to House Supervisor, Nurse Manager, Chief Nursing Officer, and now Advanced Practice, she brings a unique combination of professional expertise, leadership, compassion, and lived experience to her work.

But her greatest qualification is her testimony.

Dr. Dorothy has overcome significant adversity, including childhood challenges and becoming a single teen mother. Through prayer, faith, perseverance, healing, and determination, she transformed what could have been a story of limitation into a story of purpose and impact.

Today, she uses her voice, experience, and testimony to remind girls and young women that their beginning does not have to determine their ending.

Her vision is simple yet powerful: to help every girl discover who she is, understand whose she is, and walk boldly in her God-given purpose.

One girl. One life. One purpose at a time.

Treasurer

KaSylvia L. White

Woman of Faith

KaSylvia L. White is a dedicated, hardworking, and compassionate woman of faith. A graduate of El Centro Community College with a major in Science, she has professional experience in Invasive Cardiovascular Technology.

A God-fearing woman and lover of His Word, KaSylvia is passionate about encouraging and inspiring others to walk confidently in their God-given purpose. She is a loving mother and grandmother who treasures family above all else.

KaSylvia is honored to serve with H.E.Y. G.I.R.L., Helping Every Young Girl in "REAL LIFE," and help empower girls and young women to grow in faith, confidence, and purpose.

Fun fact: She loves traveling the world and exploring new places close to home!

Secretary

Rhonda Kay Moore

Resident Advocate · Woman of Faith

Rhonda Kay Moore is a dedicated professional, mother, grandmother, and woman of faith. A proud graduate of the Class of 1979, she has devoted 28 years to her career and has studied Social Work at Eastfield Community College and El Centro Junior College.

For more than 20 years, Rhonda has served part-time as a Resident Advocate supporting women and children impacted by domestic violence, offering compassion, encouragement, and support during challenging seasons.

She is the proud mother of one son and one daughter and grandmother of one grandson and one granddaughter. Above all, Rhonda loves God and is grateful for His grace, love, and blessings throughout her life.

Board Member

Amy McCurdy, MSN, APRN, AGPCNP-C

Nurse Practitioner · Functional & Traditional Medicine

Amy McCurdy is a nurse practitioner whose practice integrates functional and traditional medicine. She is passionate about mentoring and empowering women to recognize their strengths, trust their voices, and pursue meaningful goals with confidence, grit, grace, and a little sass, while believing it is never too late to change direction, chase a new dream, or reinvent yourself along the way.

Outside of work, she enjoys cooking, baking, staying active, traveling, laughing with loved ones, and finding joy in everyday life, preferably with good music, a little sparkle, and plenty of fun.

Board Member

Glorimer Sanchez

Social Work · Mother of Two

Glorimer Sanchez was born in Puerto Rico and moved to Texas in 2002, where she began a new chapter in her life as a single mother. She is the proud mother of two wonderful daughters. She attended college and studied social work, which reflects her passion for helping and serving others.

In her free time she enjoys reading, cooking, salsa dancing, and spending time with the people she loves. Serving others brings her joy and purpose, and she is grateful for the experiences that have shaped her into the woman and mother she is today.
